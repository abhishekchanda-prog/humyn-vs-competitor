Where each company stands

Scale AI built this category. It has enormous throughput, a broad data engine, and deep pockets. For some teams that is enough. But Meta's 49% stake in Scale, acquired in 2025, raises a question Scale's customers did not have before: who else can see the data that trains your models, and who does the team producing it answer to? Humyn Labs is built on a different premise. It's an independent multimodal human data company that owns the full pipeline, from collection through validation, multi-layer QC, annotation, and human-in-the-loop, and hands you data that is ready to train on. This page lays out where each company fits.
